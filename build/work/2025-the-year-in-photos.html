2025: The year in photos

🔗 Go to live project

✨ Design

⌨️ Development

✂️ Editing

We do a photos of the year project each year, and last year I wanted to try and do as much as I could to streamline the process for the years to come. I took stock of what would best serve our team members and editors as we moved forward through the years, and then created an Airtable database for ease of editing for our photo and copy desks.

I set up clear options in the sheet for selecting galleries versus single images, adding behind-the-lens quotes from our photographers, and more. This was our single-stop editing spot, where captions, photo paths, and even the order of images could be controlled independently of the code.

To add even more context for readers, I went through our year’s archives to find the original publication story for each photo and included that link so readers could read the original news story, too, if they wished. Even if they didn’t, having the headline for each still provided greater detail and added another layer of information for users.

Built with Svelte, Github, Google Sheets + a dream.
