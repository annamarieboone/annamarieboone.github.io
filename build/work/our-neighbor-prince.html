Our neighbor, Prince

As a transplant to Minneapolis, I've always been fascinated by the relationship between Prince and the city's residents. For the tenth anniversary of his death, I wanted to gather stories from readers of Prince the neighbor, not just Prince the rock star.

Readers responded, sending in stories that ranged from the mundane to the truly bizarre. Although it didn’t make it into the final story, my favorite part of this project was getting the chance to interview Prince’s high school music teacher. She remembered him as a shy, talented kid who dressed plainly — something that couldn’t be said for much longer after.

I created some lightweight vector illustrations to accompany each submitted story. I translated this story into an Instagram post, too.

Built with Svelte, Github, Google Sheets + a dream.
